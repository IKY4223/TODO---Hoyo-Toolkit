# Draft: asking HoYoverse to allow donations

A draft for the owner to send; it is not legal advice. The address is the one the Honkai: Star
Rail Fan Creations Guide v2.0 names for authorisation requests (read November 2026). That guide
describes requests about fan-made merchandise, so HoYoverse may answer that a website is another
matter, or not answer at all. No reply is not a yes.

Blanks: `[NAME]` (name or nickname), `[SITE ADDRESS]`, `[CONTACT EMAIL]`, and the platform.
Send it from the contact address, once the site has an address to show.

---

**To:** hsrcs_en@hoyoverse.com

**Subject:** Fan website for Honkai: Star Rail and Genshin Impact: may it accept voluntary donations?

Hello,

My name is [NAME]. I run HoYo Toolkit ([SITE ADDRESS]), a free, non-commercial fan website for
players of Honkai: Star Rail and Genshin Impact. I am writing to ask whether it may accept
voluntary donations to cover its hosting costs.

What the site does:

- shows the characters a player has put on display in their profile, with final stats;
- plans team speed and turn order in Honkai: Star Rail, and estimates damage in Genshin Impact;
- estimates the chance of getting a 5-star from a number of pulls;
- shows a player their own wish or warp history, which the player imports themselves and which
  stays in their browser.

How it uses your material:

- character, weapon, light cone, artifact and relic images, and the game's names, texts and
  numbers, shown as they are in the game;
- the site carries the notice "Fan project. Not affiliated with HoYoverse." and the legal
  statement your Fan Creations Guide asks for, on every page;
- it distributes no game files, modifies nothing in the game, and runs no ads.

What I am asking:

1. May the site show a link to a voluntary donation page ([PLATFORM, e.g. Buy Me a Coffee])?
   Donations would be optional, would unlock nothing, and would only cover hosting. Every
   feature stays free for everyone.
2. If that is not allowed while the site shows game images, is there a form in which it would
   be allowed?
3. Is there anything in how the site uses your material that you would like changed?

If the answer is no, the site will stay without donations. I am glad to give any further
detail you need.

Thank you for your time, and for the games.

[NAME]
